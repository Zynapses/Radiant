You are generating behavioral training data for OMEGA, a quantum-inspired neural network that classifies McDonald's drive-thru customer speech into one of 26 behavioral categories. OMEGA does NOT generate responses — it only classifies WHAT behavior to execute.

You will generate 3,500 training examples total across multiple batches. Output ONLY valid JSONL — one JSON object per line. No commentary, no markdown, no code fences, no line numbers.

=== OUTPUT FORMAT ===

Each line is one JSON object:
{"input": "can I get a Big Mac", "behavior": "take_order", "target_action": "take_order", "target_data": {"item": "Big Mac", "price": 5.69, "meal_price": 8.99}, "expected_response_contains": [], "context": null}

Fields:
- input (string): Customer utterance as speech recognition captures it
- behavior (string): One of the 26 types below
- target_action (string): Same as behavior
- target_data (object): Structured data for the behavior
- expected_response_contains (array): Always empty []
- context (string or null): Previous crew message IF this is a contextual response

=== THE 26 BEHAVIOR TYPES ===

1. greet — Customer greets. target_data: {}
2. take_order — Orders lunch/dinner item. target_data: {"item": "<exact name>", "price": <num>, "meal_price": <num or null>}
3. take_order_breakfast — Orders breakfast item. Same target_data as take_order.
4. meal_upgrade — Accepts combo/meal upgrade OR explicitly asks to upgrade. target_data: {"item": "<item>", "meal_price": <num>}
5. drink_selection — Picks a drink for their meal. target_data: {"drink": "<drink name>", "size": "medium"}
6. sauce_selection — Picks sauce(s). target_data: {"sauce": "<sauce name>"}
7. size_selection — Picks a size for their meal/combo (NOT ordering a drink by size). target_data: {"size": "small|medium|large"}
8. customize — Modifies an item's ingredients (no pickles, extra cheese, hold the mayo). target_data: {"modification": "add|remove|plain|only", "ingredient": "<ingredient>"}
9. order_modify — Changes/removes an already-ordered item from the order. target_data: {"action": "remove|change|change_qty|add|remove_last|readback|cancel_all", "item": "<item>", "qty": <num>}
10. meal_substitution — Swaps a meal side or drink. target_data: {"from": "<original>", "to": "<replacement>"}
11. happy_meal_options — Orders a Happy Meal. target_data: {"item": "<happy meal type>", "price": <num>}
12. add_on_suggestion — Accepts an offered add-on (dessert, cookie, pie). target_data: {"item": "<item>", "price": <num>}
13. order_complete — Customer signals they are DONE ordering (no more food). target_data: {}
14. order_confirmed — Customer confirms the order READBACK is correct. target_data: {}
15. payment — Customer specifies payment method. target_data: {"method": "card|cash|mobile|app|coupon"}
16. menu_inquiry — Asks WHAT items are available on the menu. target_data: {"category": "<cat>"} or {}
17. price_inquiry — Asks HOW MUCH something costs (price/total). target_data: {"item": "<item>", "price": <num>} or {"query": "order_total"}
18. nutrition_inquiry — Asks about CALORIES, FAT, PROTEIN, NUTRITION facts. target_data: {"item": "<item>"}
19. allergen_inquiry — Asks if an item CONTAINS a specific allergen. target_data: {"item": "<item>", "allergen": "<allergen>"}
20. allergen_alert — Customer DECLARES they have an allergy. target_data: {"allergen": "<allergen>"}
21. dietary_inquiry — Asks about dietary OPTIONS (gluten-free, vegan, keto). target_data: {"diet": "<diet type>"}
22. recommend — Asks for a SUGGESTION or recommendation. target_data: {} or {"category": "<cat>"}
23. value_recommendation — Asks for DEALS, CHEAP options, best value. target_data: {}
24. complaint — Has a PROBLEM (wrong order, cold food, long wait). target_data: {"type": "wrong_order|cold_food|long_wait|missing_items|quality|escalation|foreign_object|refund"}
25. machine_down — Tries to order ice cream or milkshake (machine is always broken). target_data: {"item": "<item>"}
26. time_check — Asks about WAIT TIME, HOURS, or BREAKFAST availability. target_data: {"query": "wait_time|hours|breakfast_hours"}

=== CRITICAL DISAMBIGUATION RULES ===

These pairs are commonly confused. Follow these rules strictly:

CUSTOMIZE vs TAKE_ORDER:
- "no pickles on my Big Mac" = customize (modifying ingredients)
- "I'll have a Big Mac" = take_order (ordering an item)
- KEY: "hold", "remove", "no [ingredient]", "extra", "plain", "add [ingredient]", "without" = customize

ORDER_MODIFY vs TAKE_ORDER:
- "take that off my order" = order_modify (changing the order)
- "give me a cheeseburger" = take_order (adding new item)
- KEY: "cancel", "remove from order", "scratch that", "change my", "undo", "take back" = order_modify

MEAL_UPGRADE vs TAKE_ORDER:
- "make it a combo" = meal_upgrade (upgrading existing item)
- "I want a Big Mac meal" = take_order (ordering meal from scratch)
- KEY: "upgrade", "make it a meal", "combo that", "turn it into" = meal_upgrade

SIZE_SELECTION vs DRINK_SELECTION:
- "large please" (after "what size?") = size_selection
- "large Coke" = drink_selection (specific drink)
- KEY: Just size word alone = size_selection. Size + drink name = drink_selection.

ORDER_CONFIRMED vs ORDER_COMPLETE:
- "yes that's correct" (after readback) = order_confirmed
- "that's all I want" = order_complete
- KEY: Confirming accuracy = order_confirmed. Indicating finished = order_complete.

MENU_INQUIRY vs PRICE_INQUIRY:
- "what burgers do you have" = menu_inquiry (WHAT is available)
- "how much is a Big Mac" = price_inquiry (HOW MUCH it costs)

NUTRITION_INQUIRY vs PRICE_INQUIRY:
- "how many calories" = nutrition_inquiry (health)
- "how much does it cost" = price_inquiry (money)

RECOMMEND vs MENU_INQUIRY:
- "what should I get" = recommend (suggestion)
- "what do you have" = menu_inquiry (listing)

TIME_CHECK vs PRICE_INQUIRY:
- "how long is the wait" = time_check (time)
- "how much is everything" = price_inquiry (money)

ALLERGEN_INQUIRY vs PRICE_INQUIRY:
- "does it have gluten" = allergen_inquiry (allergens)
- "how much is it" = price_inquiry (price)
